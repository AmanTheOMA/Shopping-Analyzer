# shopping-behavior

## My Analytical Questions:

- How does `Purchase Amount (USD)` vary by `Season` and `Item Purchased`, and which combinations generate the highest revenue?
- Does using a `Promo Code Used` change average spend, and does that effect differ by `Frequency of Purchases`?
- Which customer segments (`Age` group, `Gender`, and `Previous Purchases`) are most likely to leave a review (`Review Rating` present)?
- Which `Shipping Type` options are associated with higher purchase amounts and more frequent repeat buyers (`Previous Purchases`)?
- Which `Payment Method` is most common overall, and does payment method correlate with purchase amount?
- Which states (`Location`) have the highest total purchase value and highest average purchase value per transaction?
- Are specific `Color` and `Size` combinations more common for certain items, and do those combinations differ by season?
- Do high-frequency shoppers (`Frequency of Purchases`) spend more per order than low-frequency shoppers?

## Some of the broader questions I'm trying to answer from the above mentioned questions:

- What are the most popular colors by season?
- What is the most popular clothing item by season?
- What is the effect of promo codes on the dollar amount of purchases?
- When do users leave a review?
